How to Play

Knock the enemy leader's HP to 0. You gain 1 more gold every turn (max 10).

Drag a character card onto one of your 4 tiles to summon it. It can attack next turn (unless it has ⚡ Haste).

Drag from a glowing character to an enemy to attack. Both deal their ATK to each other — unless the attacker is 🏹 Ranged. Hitting the leader is free.

Item cards come from the novel's inventory: drag them onto a target (or anywhere, if they have no target).

Your Leader Power (bottom-left button) can be used once per turn.

You start with the Common cards. Every win earns a 🎁 card pack full of 🧩 card fragments — open it in Card Packs. Collect enough fragments of a card and it's yours: Rare cards take 4, Epic 6 and Legendary 9. Beating a Story rival adds their card at once. Right-click any card for a close-up.

👑 Bosses: the last rival of each Story chapter has a boss rule that goes off every few turns, and a 💢 rage the first time they drop to half HP. Both are shown before the fight and in the enemy's panel.

⭐ Stars: every Story stage has three stars: win, win with your leader at 20+ HP, and a challenge of its own (shown before the fight). Stars count on Normal and Hard. Each new star gives ✨ Glitter, and three stars on every stage of a chapter give an Epic Pack.

📅 Daily Missions: three new missions every day (↻ swaps one). Finishing them earns ✨ Glitter, as does every win and any pack fragment a card didn't need. Spend it in the card close-up (right-click a card): craft fragments of a card you're missing, or make a card you own Shiny.

🖼 Profile: open Profile (or click your name at the top right of the main menu) to pick a profile picture and see your stats. New pictures are won by clearing Story stages.

💞 Relationships: cards with a ♥ have a partner. When both partners are on your side of the board they fuse into one duo — new costumes, combined stats, new keywords, a special attack, and they can attack right away. Stronger bonds (♥♥, ♥♥♥) also unleash an effect when they fuse.

🔗 Item combos: some items belong to a character (cards with a 🔗). Play the item while that character is on your side of the board (aim it at them, if it targets your monsters) and they change into a combo: a new outfit, extra stats and often a bonus effect. A hand card marked COMBO has its partner on the board right now.

🂠 Decks: keep up to 3 decks in Deck & Collection (✎ renames one); the filters above the cards find what you need. Pick which deck to bring, and the difficulty, when you choose your leader.

👗 Costumes: right-click a character you own and pick an outfit from its Wardrobe.

Every character has their own attack style.
