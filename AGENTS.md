# Workspace guidance

When creating or changing a character attack animation, read and apply [the attack animation helper](.claude/skills/attack-animation/SKILL.md). This includes fixes to positioning, impact timing, and visibility in existing attacks.

When making or fixing a "<Novel> is in Miku Battle!" YouTube Short, or any video that records the game, read and apply [the novel short helper](.claude/skills/novel-short/SKILL.md). It has the tools, the commands and the rules that keep characters from vanishing out of the footage.
